Data and generation

From teleoperated real trajectories to large-scale randomized simulation streams.

RoboSynChallenge combines a smaller curated real set with a larger synthetic corpus generated using Embodied_Challenge. The site keeps both the data links and the collection protocol visible so participants can reason about what is official, what is additional, and what is still held out.

Teleoperation collection under five real-world conditions

Each task is recorded across five experimental conditions, then expanded with four position variations and three orientation settings for 60 samples per task. This gives physical correspondence for a limited but carefully structured slice of the challenge.

Official simulated data

1000 randomized trials per task, generated procedurally

The synthetic corpus contains multi-modal interaction trajectories, RGB-D observations, proprioceptive signals, structured task annotations, contact events, and success outcomes. Its main job is to extend the diversity and scale that is too expensive to collect physically.

Release policy

Open training resources, confidential test settings, and a held-out real benchmark.

What is released

We expose the simulation generator, baseline pipelines, preprocessing logic, sample trajectories, and the official dataset links. The public website is prepared to show both repository and Hugging Face entry points.

What remains held out

Final evaluation stays confidential: teams do not see test labels or raw held-out episodes. Performance is measured directly on the standardized physical setup, reducing leakage risk and overfitting to a known validation split.
